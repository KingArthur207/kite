# Follow v3: the follow and its fly brain, trained together (simulation results)

Kite's follow (`kite/follow_behind.py`, what `run/app.py` flies: 1.8 m behind, 1.9 m up, smooth2 steering yaw) was
simulated in [`kite/fly/behind`](../kite/fly/behind/DESIGN.md) with its real perception, person lock, range estimator
and SafetyGovernor, and tuned with CMA-ES on Modal in two rounds (about $99 in all). The new controller
([`kite/follow_tuned.py`](../kite/follow_tuned.py)) is the flown follow plus opt-in knobs; with its defaults it flies
bit-identically to today's follow. The flown follow, smooth2 and the retrained brain are unchanged.

| Model | File | What was tuned | Score vs the flown follow's 1.0 (train / demo / slow / fast), lower is better | Sign-off |

| **v3b-follow** | `kite/fly/data/behind/v3b-follow.json` | the follow's 25 knobs, smooth2 frozen | 0.44 / 0.44 / 0.31 / 0.46 | **passes** |

| v3-joint, v3-follow, v3-law | `kite/fly/data/behind/v3-*.json` | round 1 (from the flown follow) | 0.35-0.65 | fail: a little closer on stops |

What the trained follows do better, on 200 held-out 60 s walks per profile:
- **No fore-aft surging.** Every run learned to low-pass the "person is coming closer" back-off (~1 s): in the
  simulator, pitch-sensitive range readings set off that back-off and the flown follow rocks back and forth.
- **Closer and steadier:** median distance 3.5-4.0 m instead of 6.0 m on the demo walk; the person lost about half
  as often; the sticks move ~25 % less.
- **Safer on stops:** under 1.2 m for 0.08-0.18 s per walk on demo (flown 0.19), overshoot level with the flown
  follow, no climbing toward the ceiling (back-off climbs of 0.02-0.19 m instead of 0.25 m).
- **Speed only when needed:** below the person-speed gate the forward cap stays at or below the flown 35. The cap
  can rise to 51-52 (~0.5 m/s) when the person is measured walking faster.

Limits, said plainly:
- **The speed-up rarely engages in the simulator.** The person-speed estimate rides on the same noisy range, so the
  gate seldom opens: along a 0.6 m/s stretch the drone averages ~0.15 m/s, like the flown follow. Every follow still
  trails the 0.35 m/s demo walk by metres in the simulator.
- **The simulator's range noise is not yet checked against a flight log.** It drives most of the difference
  between the follows. One real follow behind a slow walker (telemetry pitch, estimated range, forward stick) settles
  whether the simulator is too pessimistic.
- **The v3b models ask for stick 51-52 when the gate opens;** the app's SafetyCfg clamps every axis at 50.
- Simulation only: each model stays opt-in until a real flight next to smooth2.

The full sign-off follows.
